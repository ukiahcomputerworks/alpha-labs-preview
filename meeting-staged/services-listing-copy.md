## Our Testing & Analytical Services

*Certified environmental testing for Northern California.*

- [**Drinking & Bottled Water**](https://ukiahcomputerworks.github.io/alpha-labs-preview/drinking-bottled-water-program/)

  Certified testing for public systems, private wells, and bottled water. We screen for bacteria, trace metals, and organics using dedicated GC, GCMS, and HPLC instruments required for **Title 21, Title 22, and Disinfectant By-Product compliance analyses**.

- [**Wastewater, Recycled & Storm Water**](https://ukiahcomputerworks.github.io/alpha-labs-preview/wastewater-recycled-water-storm-water-ground-water-program-work/)

  NPDES, industrial, and municipal support. We handle landfill sludge characterization and meet the strict low-level reporting limits of the California Toxics Rule (CTR).

- [**POTW Pretreatment Programs**](https://ukiahcomputerworks.github.io/alpha-labs-preview/potw-pretreatment-program-work/)

  Regulatory compliance and field sampling for POTW Discharge Permits. Services include 24-hour auto-sampler installations and onsite grab sampling. Questions? Email **robbie@alpha-labs.com**.

- [**Soil, Sediment & Hazardous Waste**](https://ukiahcomputerworks.github.io/alpha-labs-preview/soil-sludge-sediment-haz-waste-characterization/)

  Full SW846 testing for groundwater monitoring, UST compliance, and site remediation. We also provide hazardous waste screens and trout survival bioassays.

> **WaterTrax Partner:** We report results directly into [**WaterTrax**](http://www.watertrax.com/) for secure, seamless utility data management.
